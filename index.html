STEP 01 + 02

Fill the nine boxes, in this order. Mark each one as you go.

Keep every box to a line or two. If a box takes a paragraph, it isn’t clear enough yet. Then mark it: Known means you can name something that happened outside your own head. Assumed means you believe it, with reasons, and no evidence. Unknown means you hadn’t thought about it until now.

The money path · boxes 1 to 5

Who, what, how they find you, how you keep them, how you get paid. This is the side most owners have never had to prove, so it comes first.

What it takes to deliver · boxes 6 to 9

You can’t size delivery until you know what you’re delivering and to whom. Fill these last.

STEP 03

Name your riskiest box.

The question

Which one box, if you have it wrong, makes the other eight irrelevant? That’s a different question from which box you’re least sure about. Almost every time, it’s customer segments. Not always, but start there.

Name the boxes that were built on top of it.

STEP 04

Write the test. Thirty days. Somebody has to be able to say no.

A test is something a real person does that costs them money, time or a signature, and that could come back no. Research tells you what people think. A test tells you what they do.

Real people who fit the box you’re testing.

Something that costs them money, time or a signature.

The result that tells you the box is wrong.

Within thirty days.

Write it so someone else could run it.

After the test · for your next version
